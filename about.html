Bridging the Gap Between College and Real Silicon

If you've studied Electronics, Electrical, or Computer Engineering in college, you already know the disconnect: you spend years writing basic 8051 code or memorizing textbook equations, only to graduate and realize the industry has moved to 32-bit ARM Cortex, RISC-V, FreeRTOS, high-speed PCB routing, and nanometer silicon fabrication.

Students interested in hardware and embedded systems often struggle for years without direction—spending months trying to figure out what skills companies actually test, what kind of projects prove competence, and how working engineers actually built their careers.

SiliconCraft was built so that you don't have to wander blindly in the dark for years like I had to.

What Happens Here

🎙️ Conversations with Working Professionals

We speak with engineers, researchers, founders, and technical leaders from semiconductor giants, tier-1 automotive suppliers, and hardware startups. They share their real career journeys, hiring realities, and what they actually look for during technical rounds.

📐 Practical Industry Insights

No generic web-dev fluff. In-depth technical articles covering register-level programming, DMA circular buffers, FreeRTOS priority inversion, oscilloscope debugging, and hardware trade-offs.

🛠️ Real Projects & Community Help

A central space to share verified hardware builds with complete bills of materials (BOM), schematics, and firmware repositories. When your UART drops packets or your I2C bus hangs, you have a community of people ready to help you debug.

💼 Direct Opportunities & Challenges

Curated internship openings, entry-level fresher roles, and national hardware hackathons filtered strictly for electronics, firmware, and silicon roles.

In short: A platform built to help the next generation of hardware and embedded engineers find the guidance you wish you had.

Whether you are struggling with your first microcontroller project or preparing for your first semiconductor interview, this platform is for you.
